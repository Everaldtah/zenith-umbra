Support

ZENITH UMBRA and TermForge for iPhone and iPad, by Everald Tah.

To report a bug, ask a question or request a feature, open an issue on GitHub. Say which app and version you use and which iPhone or iPad model. A screenshot helps.

ZENITH UMBRA

  • Report a problem or ask a question: github.com/Everaldtah/zenith-umbra/issues
  • Controls: the on-screen controller hides when a controller connects. Pair a DualShock 4 (hold SHARE + PS) or a DualSense (CREATE + PS) under Settings > Bluetooth. Adjust the touch controls under Options > Controls > Touch & Controller.
  • Saved highlights are in the Files app under On My iPhone / On My iPad > ZENITH UMBRA.

TermForge

  • Report a problem or ask a question: github.com/Everaldtah/termforge/issues
  • Documentation and known limitations: github.com/Everaldtah/termforge
  • Your files are in the Files app under On My iPhone / On My iPad > TermForge, and inside the Linux environment at /mnt/termforge.

Privacy

Neither app collects personal data. Read the privacy policy.
