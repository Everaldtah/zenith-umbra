Privacy Policy

Synapse Notes for iPhone and iPad, by Everald Tah. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Synapse is a notes, documents and databases app with optional AI features. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The developer runs no server that receives your data and never sees your notes, recordings, files or API keys.

Stored on your device

  • Your workspace: pages, databases, comments, attachments, recordings, transcripts and files made by agents. They are kept in the app's container, which you can open in the Files app under Synapse.
  • API keys and the Synapse Bridge pairing token, in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your settings, including which AI services you have allowed.
  • AI models you download and the Linux system, if you install it.

Deleting the app removes all of this. You can also export your workspace as Markdown at any time (Settings > Export Markdown vault).

Processed on your device

By default, AI features run on the device with Apple Intelligence or with an open model you download. Recordings are transcribed on the device with Apple's speech recognition. None of this leaves the device.

When data leaves your device

Synapse connects to the internet only when you, or an agent you asked to do something, start a request:

  • Cloud AI services you choose. If you pick a cloud model, your request goes to that service: what you type, the page text and files you include, and any images or audio you attach. This can be an OpenAI-compatible provider (for example NVIDIA NIM, Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or Mistral, or a server of your own), Anthropic's Claude API with your own key, or Synapse Bridge on your own computer, which passes the request to Claude Code or Codex under your own Anthropic or OpenAI account. Before the first request to each service, Synapse asks your permission and names the service. Nothing is sent if you decline. The service's own privacy policy applies, and some free services may keep or log prompts. You can make Synapse ask again under Settings > Privacy.
  • The web. When you or an agent search the web or open a page, the search terms go to the search service (DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia or Google) and the page is loaded from its website, as in any browser.
  • Downloads. AI models are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). The Linux system is downloaded from the project's GitHub releases, and packages you install in it come from their own repositories.
  • Synapse Bridge. If you pair the app with Synapse Bridge, it connects only to the computer you paired, over your own network.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan the bridge's pairing QR code, take photos for your pages and scan documents.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to record meetings and voice notes and transcribe them on the device.
  • Local network: to reach Synapse Bridge on your computer.

Each permission is requested only when you first use the feature, and can be changed in the iOS Settings app.

Children

Synapse is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes what is described here, this page will be updated before that version is released. Questions about privacy: see the support page.
