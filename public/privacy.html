Privacy Policy

ZENITH UMBRA and TermForge for iPhone and iPad, by Everald Tah. Last updated 26 October 2026.

Neither app collects personal data. Neither has developer accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and neither sends anything about you or your device to the developer or to any third party for the developer's use.

ZENITH UMBRA

ZENITH UMBRA is an offline hero shooter. It makes no connections to servers on the internet.

Stored on your device

  • Game settings, controls and your career statistics.
  • Highlight videos you choose to save. They are kept in the app's Documents folder, which you can open in the Files app and delete at any time.

Game controllers

If you connect a PlayStation, Xbox or other controller, the game reads its buttons, battery level and model name on the device to play the game and drive the light bar and rumble. None of this leaves the device.

Deleting the app removes all of its data.

TermForge

TermForge is a terminal that runs tools on your iPhone or iPad. The developer runs no servers and receives no data from the app. Network connections happen only when you start them:

  • Installing Claude Code. The app downloads the package from the npm registry (registry.npmjs.org). npm's own privacy policy applies to that download.
  • The Linux environment. On first use the app downloads its Alpine Linux root file system from the project's GitHub releases. Commands you run in the terminal, such as apk, git or curl, connect to the servers you point them at.
  • Claude Code and the Agent tab. When you sign in to Claude Code or enter an Anthropic API key, the prompts you type and the files you share with the model are sent to Anthropic under your own account. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that data. The developer never sees it.
  • The PC tab. It connects only to the computer you pair it with, over your own network.

Stored on your device

  • Your files, the Linux file system, installed packages and terminal history, in the app's container.
  • API keys, sign-in tokens and pairing codes, in the iOS Keychain.

Deleting the app removes all of its data, including its Keychain items.

Children

Neither app knowingly collects data from anyone, including children.
